# **Product Name: PING** 

## **PRODUCT OVERVIEW:** 

Ping is a problem-solving platform that lets people flag and report issues in seconds and follows it through from the beginning to the end (report to resolution). Reporters are always aware of the situation of the problem they reported, teams get a clearer prioritized queue instead of scattered emails and texts. 

## **THE PROBLEM IT SOLVES:** 

- Issues get reported from a lot of channels (calls, whatsapp, emails, papers, in person) thereby creating an avenue for mistakes and ignoring some. 

- Reporters never get a reply or hear back so they stop reporting. 

- Teams can’t see patterns, so the same problem keeps recurring. 

- Students don’t know who to report to, so they say nothing. 

## **KEY FEATURES:** 

- Fast reporting: it’ll adopt a short form with photo, location (hostel block, lecture hall, library, caf, e.t.c) could be anywhere in school. 

- Auto-routing: These reports go to the right person or team base on category and location 

- Status tracking: Received>Assigned>In progress>Resolved, with notifications at each major step 

- Timers: Urgent issues surface first and overdue ones re-appear. 

- Two-way Updates: Comments and follow-up questions for both reporter and resolver 

## **WHO IT’S FOR:** 

- Students who have a complaint 

- Faculty operations teams 

- Organizations collecting customer complaints 

## **WHY PING?** 

- Faster resolution times 

- Higher level of trust since reporters see the progress along the line. 

- Stored Data to resolve future issues. 

## **MVP FEATURES:** 

Report submissions, a team inbox with assignment, status updates with notifications, and a basic dashboard.
